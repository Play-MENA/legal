Data Deletion Request

Last updated: June 10, 2026

This page explains how to delete the data associated with PlayMENA games. It currently applies to Hexa Puzzle and will cover future PlayMENA games as they are released.

Contents

  2. Delete It Yourself

1. What Data We Hold

Our games can be played anonymously, without creating an account. You may optionally connect with Facebook to save and sync your progress (Apple and Google sign-in may be added later). Depending on how you play, we may hold:

  • On-device data: your game progress and settings, stored locally on your device.
  • Advertising & analytics data: your device advertising ID and usage/crash data collected through Google AdMob and Google Firebase / Google Analytics.
  • Connected-account data (only if you link Facebook): your saved progress and the basic profile information Facebook shares with us (such as your name and Facebook account identifier), used to sync your progress across devices.

2. Delete It Yourself

You can remove most data on your own at any time:

  • Delete local progress: uninstall the game, or clear its storage via your device settings (Android: Settings → Apps → Hexa Puzzle → Storage → Clear data).
  • Disconnect Facebook: if you linked Facebook, disconnect it from within the game to stop syncing your progress. You can also remove the app's access from your Facebook Apps and Websites settings.
  • Reset your advertising ID: in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking) to limit ad tracking.

3. Request a Full Deletion

To request deletion of any data we hold about you — including Facebook-linked progress and analytics data — email us at playmena.labs@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". Please include:

  • The game name (e.g. Hexa Puzzle);
  • Whether you played anonymously or connected with Facebook;
  • If you connected Facebook, the name on your Facebook account (and, if possible, send the request from the email linked to that account) so we can locate your data.

We will confirm and complete your request within 30 days.

4. What We Delete & Retain

Upon a verified request, we delete the progress and profile data linked to your Facebook account and disassociate analytics data tied to your identifiers. We may retain limited information where required by law, for fraud prevention, or in routine backups, which are overwritten on a rolling basis. Anonymous, aggregated statistics that cannot identify you may be retained.

5. Contact

Questions about deleting your data? Email playmena.labs@gmail.com.
